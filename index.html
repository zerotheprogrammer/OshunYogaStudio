Welcome to Oshun Yoga Studio, where movement, mindfulness, and inner balance flow together. Inspired by Oshun, the Yoruba goddess associated with fresh water, love, beauty, and prosperity, our studio offers a warm and peaceful space to reconnect with your body and mind. Surrounded by calming touches of gold, we invite you to slow down, breathe deeply, and discover your own strength through the practice of yoga. Whether you are stepping onto the mat for the first time or continuing your yoga journey, Oshun Yoga Studio welcomes you to Find Your Flow. Embrace Your Light.

  • Hatha, Vinyasa, and Restorative Yoga classes
  • Drop-ins welcome
  • Mats, blocks, and blankets provided
  • Relax in our Serenity Lounge before or after your class
